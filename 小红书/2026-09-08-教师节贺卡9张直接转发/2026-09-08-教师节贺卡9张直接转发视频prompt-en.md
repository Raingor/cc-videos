# September 8, 2026 · JokeBear Teacher's Day Cards | Video Content Prompts

> Nine connected vertical scenes, corresponding one-to-one with the nine greeting cards. Do not generate text in the images; add card copy and signature lines afterward.

## Shared visual direction

One consistent white 3D JokeBear in every scene: a wide rounded oval head, small round body, short oval arms and legs, no visible fingers or toes, tiny black dot eyes, a small black nose, and a compact closed omega-shaped mouth. No eyebrows, blush, teeth, clothing, or accessories. Keep its shape, proportions, material, and scale identical across all nine scenes.

The visual style is a clean, restrained 3D greeting-card animation: soft diffused lighting, low-saturation cream pastels, generous negative space, little to no depth of field, and a fixed vertical composition. Cards and reserved blank areas must remain clear. Use only subtle natural motion in each scene: a gentle breathing bob, one blink, and very small head or arm movements. Keep the camera locked; no zoom, pan, tilt, dramatic transition, or visual effect.

Do not show words, letters, numbers, watermarks, logos, real school names, school emblems, people, or additional characters. Avoid furry texture, realistic bears, exaggerated expressions, hands with fingers, cluttered classrooms, and busy backgrounds.

## Narrative continuity

JokeBear enters the classroom carrying a blank greeting card. It remembers a moment at the blackboard, the red corrections on its homework, and the patient process of improving. It turns that gratitude into a stack of notebooks, a warm glass of water, and an unspoken thank-you, then ends at sunset with a card ready for a signature. All scenes take place in the same classroom, with the same bear and compatible soft light. The final object, eyeline, or motion of each scene must become the visual entry point for the next. Use natural action cuts and continuous colors only; no flash, spin, fast push-in, or effect transition.

## 01 | Cover: Bear brings the first card

```text
Vertical 3:4. A quiet warm cream classroom doorway in soft afternoon sunlight from a side window. A single small pot of white flowers sits on the windowsill. White 3D JokeBear faces the camera, standing slightly below center and holding a rounded warm-orange greeting card with both paws. The card carries only a tiny minimal flower illustration, with no text. Leave a large clean cream-white area in the upper half for later title placement, plus a small blank area at lower right. JokeBear gently raises the card, blinks once, then looks down at it and walks toward the desk inside the classroom. End with the warm-orange card near the center of the frame, ready to connect to the blank card in the next scene. Sincere, soft, and immediately shareable as a Teacher's Day card.
```

## 03 | Remembering those words

```text

In the background is a clean dark green blackboard with one faint chalk stroke only, no writing or equations. A simple desk sits below. White 3D JokeBear sits at the desk, angled slightly toward the blackboard and looking quietly at the chalk stroke, as if recalling something a teacher once said. The bear blinks slowly and lifts its head a little, then lowers its gaze to the homework notebook on the desk. Match the position of the faint chalk stroke to a red correction mark on the notebook in the next scene. Keep open space on the upper or right side; sincere and restrained.
```

## 04 | Thank you for not giving up on me

```text
Vertical 3:4. Continue naturally from the desk-level eyeline into a wooden desk scene inside a warm beige card border with soft red accents. White 3D JokeBear sits at the desk, looking down at an open homework notebook. It contains only abstract red correction strokes, tiny red check marks, and circles, never readable words or numbers. One paw rests lightly on the edge of the notebook while the other stays near its chest. The bear looks down, then slowly lifts its head with quiet gratitude. It gently moves one small red circular correction sticker to the edge of the desk, where three plain small blocks are already waiting. End on the blocks to lead into the next scene. Leave soft blank space above; warm but never overly sentimental.
```

## 05 | It is okay to improve slowly

## 06 | Corrected homework becomes confidence

```text

The bear begins slightly strained by the stack, then holds it securely, straightens a little, and looks calm and confident. The uppermost notebook edge makes one small movement before becoming still. ## 07 | Teachers should rest too

```text

No handouts, nameplates, or other objects are present. After setting it down, the bear steps back half a step, looks up at the glass, and makes one quiet nod. Leave clean pale space above the podium, like a softly spoken wish of care.
```

```

## 09 | Final card: space for a signature
